🖨️ How to Print at the Library

A Guide to using the Eagle Print system

  1. Log in to a computer
    Sign in to any library computer using your student username and password.
  2. Add funds to Eagle Print
    Minimize your open windows to locate the green Eagle Print balance widget on the desktop. Click the yellow "Details" link, enter your password, and add funds using a credit card.
    (Note: If you are paying with cash, you can use the cash machine located near the printer instead.)
    Printing Rates:
    • Black & White: $0.10 per page
    • Color: $0.50 per page
  3. Send to printer
    Print your document and select the Eagle Print printer from the drop-down menu.
  4. Release your document
    Walk over to the printer. The separate print release screen is located directly on the machine. Log in by typing your username and password on the screen.
  5. Print and pick up
    Select your print job on the display screen, click "Print" to release it, and collect your documents from the tray.

📱 Add Library Nest to Your Home Screen

Access library resources instantly from your phone or tablet by saving this web app!

iOS (iPhone / iPad)

  1. Tap the Share button (the box with an upward arrow) in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the Three Dots menu icon in the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Follow the onscreen prompt to confirm.
